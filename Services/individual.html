Program 01 · Individual Coaching

You are carrying more than anyone can see. It does not have to stay that way.

You want to lead well, decide well, and feel like yourself again while you do it. But right now something is in the way.

A business decision you keep circling. A relationship that has gone quiet or gone tense. A way of operating that used to work and does not anymore.

Left alone, this does not resolve itself. It compounds. The same patterns keep repeating, the same weight keeps landing on you, and a year from now you are tired in the same way, just more so.

The Guide

You do not have to carry this by yourself.

Allison knows what it is like to be the one everyone depends on. She has spent her career in rooms with real stakes: twenty years in the oil industry, ten years on a board of education, and eleven years developing leaders, teams, and partnerships in high stakes, high confrontation environments. What that taught her is simple. The weight does not get lighter by trying harder.

This is coaching where you stay in the driver's seat. You decide what we work on. Allison helps you see it clearly and find what actually moves you forward.

The Plan

Three simple steps.

01

Bring what is live for you

A business issue. A strained relationship. A leadership pattern you are ready to change. You choose.

02

Work it together, every two weeks for a year

Biweekly calls, plus unlimited support by email, text, or phone whenever you need it, not just when a session happens to be scheduled.

03

Move forward with a clearer head and steadier hands

Better decisions. Repaired relationships. A way of leading that does not require you to white knuckle your way through it.
