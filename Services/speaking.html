The Problem Your Audience Brings Into the Room

They are not burned out from lack of effort. They are burned out from leading alone.

Most of the leaders in your audience are carrying more than their share. They are working harder than ever and still feel like they are the only ones who care. They have great people around them who are not stepping up. They have tried to change things before and somehow ended up right back in the same place.

They have never had a model for seeing exactly what is driving that pattern. And they have never had a process for actually changing it. That is what they will leave with.

At a recent leadership development day, every single outcome measure showed a significant shift in a single session. Attendees rated their confidence and capability before and after, and across every category the average score jumped from around 2.5 to nearly 4 out of 5.

And when asked what they would do differently as a result of the session, they did not write vague intentions. They wrote specific plans:

Share the quiz with our team.
Call it out when no one is responding in a meeting.
Be more conscious of confidence busters.
Have difficult conversations I have been avoiding.

The most common suggestion for improvement? More time.

The Workshop, In Depth

A half day or full day built around your team's real challenge.

A workshop is not a shorter keynote and it does not require one. It stands on its own. You bring a group, a real challenge, and a few hours. Allison builds the rest around it.

Workshops are built from the topics below, the wider set of challenges in leadership and partnership beyond what the keynote covers. The work itself is not a lecture. Teams work through their actual situation in the room, not a hypothetical one, and they leave having already done the hard part once, together, instead of carrying it home to figure out alone.

Half day

For one topic, one team, one real shift.

Full day

For teams ready to go deeper, often combining two related topics or working through a single challenge from multiple angles.

Every workshop is built around your team, your context, and the challenge you are actually facing. Tell Allison what is going on and she will build the format and topic combination that fits.
